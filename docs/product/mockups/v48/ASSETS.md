## 2–4. Inflation, Jobs, Housing — NOT VERIFIED, placeholders retained

### What was searched

- Carol M. Highsmith Archive (verified copyright-free) for `grocery store produce`,
  `supermarket`, `farmers market`, `factory`, `factory workers manufacturing`,
  `steel mill worker`, `construction site`, `houses under construction subdivision`,
  `new homes`.
- Library of Congress photo search filtered to `rights: no known restrictions`
  for `grocery`, `factory`, `housing construction`.

### Why nothing was adopted

**The Highsmith archive is architecture and landscape, not people at work or
retail interiors.** Its `steel mill` results are all *idle and abandoned*
plants — placing one on a "Jobs" tile would imply industrial decline, which is
a claim about the economy that nothing in the product supports.

**The wider no-known-restrictions pool is historical.** Grocery results dated
1880, 1895, 1897, 1930, 1940; housing-construction results 1939–1950. On a page
whose entire claim is current, sourced data, an undated 1940 photograph of a
grocery store is a contextual misrepresentation. It could be used *with a
visible date caption* — that is a legitimate editorial direction, and a
different one from the contemporary look that was approved.

### On the four images supplied with this request

None can be adopted, and two fail on their face regardless of licence:

| | Observation | Blocking issue |
|---|---|---|
| Grocery | Identifiable woman, face clear; store sign "FAIR WINDS"; a price tag reading "$1.49/LB LG BEEFSTEAK TOMATOES" | Model release required; property release likely; **and a legible current price on an inflation tile reads as a data claim the product is not making** |
| Factory | Identifiable worker; shirt carries a legible third-party trademark, "Justrite Safety Group" | Model release required; **a real company's branding on our homepage implies a relationship that does not exist** |
| Treasury at dusk | Heavily processed sky, commercial-stock characteristics | No provenance supplied. A photograph of a federal building is copyrighted **by the photographer**, not public domain |
| Aerial subdivision | No identifiable people (good); drone/stock characteristics | No provenance supplied |

No source URL, creator or rights statement came with any of them, and none was
reverse-verifiable from the pixels. **I did not put any of them in the
prototype.**

## Acceptance rules for any candidate

1. Documented commercial licence held by us, web use, indefinite term.
2. No identifiable person without a model release; no identifiable private
   property without a property release.
3. No third-party trademarks, brand packaging or signage.
4. Not AI-generated unless that can be stated in the credit line.
5. Credit line stored with the file and rendered if required.
6. Must survive the treatment: dark tint, white text along the bottom edge.
7. **No legible price, figure or date in frame** unless we intend to stand
   behind it. This rule is new, and the supplied grocery image is why.
